**Role:** Lead Researcher / AI Research Fellow
**Timeline:** Jan 2025 – Present (Presented 2025)
**Tools:** Python, DistilBERT, Rasch Item Response Theory (IRT) analysis, NLP pipeline

Engineered an NLP and psychometric pipeline to evaluate the efficacy of Emory University's novel Chemistry Unbound curriculum. Combined DistilBERT sentiment classification with Rasch IRT modeling to analyze non-binary datasets of student assessment performance and feedback text — replacing a manual review process that previously took 50+ hours per cycle.

**Honors:** Best Poster Award & Emory AI X.perience Fellowship
