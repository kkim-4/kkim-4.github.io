**Role:** Lead Sports Analytics Researcher
**Timeline:** Jan 2025 – Present (Presented 2025)
**Tools:** Python, XGBoost, Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLPNN), CNN, feature engineering, spatiotemporal data analysis

Developed a sports analytics stacked-ensemble machine learning architecture to forecast explosive NFL runs. Engineered custom spatiotemporal features and trained base-level models — XGBoost, MLPNN, and CNN — combined with a shallow XGBoost meta-learner, reaching 0.71 AUC and outperforming other contemporary models.

**Honors:** Best Poster Award & Emory AI X.perience Fellowship
