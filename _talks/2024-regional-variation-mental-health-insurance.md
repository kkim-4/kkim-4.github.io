**Role:** Health Economics Research Assistant & Presenter
**Timeline:** Sep 2022 – 2024 (Presented 2024)
**Tools:** R, Python, econometric modeling, MEPS (Medical Expenditure Panel Survey), AHRF (Area Health Resources Files)

Compiled and analyzed national healthcare databases (MEPS, AHRF) to build econometric models examining regional disparities in mental health provider insurance acceptance and reimbursement rates across the United States. Presented findings at the 2024 SRSA conference to highlight mental health parity law gaps and access inequality.
