**Stack:** Python, PyMC / Stan, Bayesian statistics, state-space models, data analytics
**Timeline:** Aug 2026 – Present
**Role:** Lead Data Scientist & Researcher

Developed a Dual-Process Bayesian State-Space Model to rigorously evaluate NFL kicker performance in high-pressure scenarios. The model processes fine-grained situational context — kick distance, clock pressure, score differential, and career volume — to compute a normalized Field Goal Over Expected (FGOE) metric. By isolating situational difficulty from athlete variance, the system distinguishes high-risk misses driven by environmental factors from true latent skill degradation over time.
