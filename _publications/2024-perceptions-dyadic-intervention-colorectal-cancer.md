**Role:** Co-Author & Qualitative Research Assistant
**Timeline:** May 2023 – Sep 2023 (Published 2024)
**Tools:** Dedoose, qualitative analysis, data collection/entry

Co-authored a published clinical qualitative study investigating technology-based dyadic interventions for psychoneurological symptom management in colorectal cancer patients and their caregivers. Screened dozens of patients, collected and entered study data, and led qualitative coding using Dedoose to directly inform the software architecture of a web-based clinical intervention.
