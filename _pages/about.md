I'm an M.S. student in Computer Science and Engineering at UC San Diego, starting in September 2026. I recently completed my B.S. in Neuroscience with a minor in Computer Science at Emory University. My research centers on multi-agent LLM systems, machine metacognition, and AI evaluation — with a growing interest in how these systems fail, and how to measure that failure rigorously.

I'm currently a Software Engineering Intern at Altasciences, where I'm prototyping an LLM pipeline that turns clinical report templates and study data into populated first drafts. I'm also a Visiting Researcher in Human-Centered AI at UIUC, studying LLM sycophancy through a longitudinal field study, and a Research Assistant at Emory working on multi-agent topologies for metacognitive AI systems, advised by Dr. Kai Shu.

Before that, I spent a year and a half as an AI Research Fellow at the Emory Center for AI Learning, where I built NLP pipelines for curriculum evaluation and sports analytics models for the NFL — including a stacked-ensemble model predicting explosive run plays and a Bayesian state-space model for kicker performance. I also worked for several years as a Health Economics Research Assistant, studying disparities in mental health access using national survey data.
