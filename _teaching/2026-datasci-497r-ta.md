I served as a teaching assistant for DATASCI 497R at the Emory Center for AI Learning, mentoring undergraduate researchers through the full arc of independent projects — from scoping a question to presenting results.

Mentoring
======
Mentored 30+ undergraduate researchers across the semester, helping each student scope a tractable research question, design a defensible methodology, and prepare their final presentation.

Workshops
======
Designed and delivered lessons and workshops on Python, PyTorch, and ML tooling for students new to the stack, covering everything from basic scripting to training their first models.

Sponsor partnerships
======
Partnered with external project sponsors, including Invest Atlanta and the Nell Hodgson Woodruff School of Nursing, to translate their real-world questions into tractable, data-backed analyses for students to pursue.
