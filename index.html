Questions I'm exploring

Studying how the brain learns, decides, and second-guesses itself left me with a question I still can't shake.

That question drives most of what I build. My main focus is metacognitive LLM multi-agent systems: teams of agents that track their own confidence and reliability, so they can tell when to trust a teammate, when to defer, and when to admit uncertainty.

More recently I've been bringing these ideas to agentic recommender systems, which turn out to be a great sandbox for modeling messy, shifting human preferences.

What would it take for AI to know what it doesn't know?
